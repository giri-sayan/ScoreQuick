# Privacy Policy for ScoreQuick

**Last Updated:** October 4, 2026

**ScoreQuick** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure for the ScoreQuick browser extension.

---

### 1. Single Purpose & Data Collection
ScoreQuick is built with a **strict privacy-first philosophy**. Its single purpose is to provide real-time live sports scores, standings, schedules, and alerts for Football, Cricket, and Formula 1.

- **No Personal Data Collected**: We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, IP address, or payment details).
- **No Browsing History or Activity Tracking**: We do **not** track your browsing history, web traffic, clicks outside the extension popup, or keystrokes.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking Cookies**: ScoreQuick does not include third-party tracking scripts, analytics engines (like Google Analytics), or behavioral advertising SDKs.

---

### 2. Permissions & How They Are Used
ScoreQuick requests minimal browser permissions strictly required to provide its core features:

- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to store your followed teams, drivers, selected sport filters, and refresh interval preferences **locally on your device**. This data never leaves your browser.
- **`alarms`**: Used to schedule background polling intervals to fetch live match score updates.
- **`notifications`**: Used to display desktop alerts when a followed team scores a goal, a wicket falls, or a match concludes.
- **`tabs`**: Used solely to open authentic match center links or sports news articles in a new tab when clicked by the user.
- **`host_permissions`**: Used to fetch live match scores, telemetry, and official team logos directly from public sports endpoints (e.g. FotMob, CREX, Formula 1).

---

### 3. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all user settings and followed teams are stored locally in your browser via `chrome.storage.local`, you have complete control over your data. You can clear your followed teams anytime within the extension settings or by uninstalling the extension, which automatically deletes all locally stored data.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Links
The extension displays live data and provides links to public sports sources (e.g. FotMob, CREX, Formula 1, BuyMeACoffee). When you click an external link, you are directed to that third party's website, which is governed by their respective privacy policies.

---

### 5. Developer Program Policies Compliance
We certify that:
- We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
- We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
- We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
